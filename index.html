CURATED JOURNEY PROCESS

THE 8-DAY EXPEDITION ROADMAP

A sequential learning journey bridging classroom science with the world's most advanced aerospace, robotics, high-speed transit, and cultural capitals of Japan.

01
Day 01 • Arrival & Tech

Sensoji Temple & Akihabara

Explore Tokyo's oldest temple Nakamise-dori in Asakusa, followed by Akihabara's world-famous consumer robotics and electronics culture.

arrival in tokyo & robotics markets ✈️
02
Day 02 • Aerospace

JAXA Space Agency & Tsukuba

Tour Japan's aerospace headquarters in Tsukuba Science City, inspect 50m orbital rockets, satellite cleanrooms, and ISS Kibo control.

50m orbital rockets & kibo control 🚀
03
Day 03 • AI & Robotics

Miraikan Science & Tokyo Tower

Interact with humanoid androids and the Geo-Cosmos globe at Japan's premier museum, followed by 360° skyline views from Tokyo Tower.

meet humanoid androids at miraikan 🤖
04
Day 04 • Automation

Kawasaki Robots & Haneda City

Hands-on encounters with industrial duAro robots at Kawasaki Robostage, and explore smart city mobility at Haneda Innovation City.

industrial duAro robotics hands-on 🦾
05
Day 05 • High-Speed Rail

Shinkansen Train & Lake Ashi Cruise

Ride the 320 km/h Shinkansen bullet train to Hakone, marvel at Mount Fuji (3,776m), and cruise across Lake Ashi aboard an authentic pirate ship.

320 km/h bullet train & mt. fuji view 🚅
06
Day 06 • Exchange

School Cultural Exchange & Dining

Cross-cultural exchange with Japanese students, comparing STEM curricula and ideas, followed by an evening at Tokyo's iconic robot dining show.

cross-cultural student bonding 🎓
07
Day 07 • Engineering & Magic

Tokyo Disneyland • Full Day Pass

A magical full day at Tokyo Disneyland. Experience state-of-the-art animatronics, engineering marvels, parades, and unforgettable student bonding.

state-of-the-art animatronics & rides 🏰
08
Day 08 • Departure

Tokyo Highlights & Return to India

Souvenir shopping in Ginza, certificate ceremony commemorating tour completion, and final transfer to Tokyo International Airport for the flight home.

souvenir shopping & certificate ceremony ✈️
