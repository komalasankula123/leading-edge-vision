🇯🇵 Educational Delegation • 8 Days / 7 Nights

8-Day Japan STEM & Robotics Schedule

Comprehensive day-by-day learning roadmap covering JAXA Space Agency, Miraikan Robotics, Kawasaki Robostage, Mount Fuji Shinkansen, and Tokyo Disneyland.

DAY 01

Arrival in Tokyo • Heritage & Tech

●
Sensoji Temple (Asakusa): Tokyo's oldest 7th-century temple and traditional Nakamise-dori shopping lane.
●
Akihabara Electronics City: The world-famous hub for micro-components, sensors, gadgets, and consumer robotics.
DAY 02

Space & Science Exploration

●
JAXA Space Agency: Tour Tsukuba Space Center, actual H-II launch vehicles, real satellites, and ISS KIBO astronaut module.
●
Tsukuba Science City: Interactive physics experiments and premier national research institutes shaping the future.
DAY 03

Innovation & City Views

●
Miraikan Science Museum: Interactive AI algorithms, humanoid robotics demonstrations, quantum computing exhibits, and Geo-Cosmos.
●
Tokyo Tower Observatory: Panoramic 360-degree views of Tokyo skyline and Tokyo Bay from the iconic 333m tower.
DAY 04

Robotics & Future Innovation

●
Kawasaki Robot Museum: Hands-on encounters with duAro collaborative industrial, surgical, and automotive robots in action.
●
Haneda Innovation City: Cutting-edge smart mobility testbeds, AI research facilities, and immersive deep-tech incubators.
DAY 05
🚄 Bullet Train Day

Mount Fuji & Scenic Hakone

●
Mount Fuji (3,776m): Explore UNESCO-listed Fuji Five Lakes and high-altitude volcanic geological marvels.
●
Lake Ashi Pirate Cruise: Scenic cruise aboard replica galleon followed by high-speed Shinkansen Bullet Train transit.
DAY 06

School Exchange & Robot Tech

●
Tokyo School Cultural Exchange: Interactive peer-to-peer STEM sessions with Japanese students, building global connections.
●
Robot Restaurant Experience: Dinner surrounded by futuristic robotic animatronics, laser choreography, and tech shows.
DAY 07
🏰 Magic & Tech

Tokyo Disneyland — Magic & Engineering

●
1-Day Disneyland Passport: Experience world-renowned theme park physics, trackless animatronics, and ride engineering.
●
Electrical Night Parade: Advanced multimedia illumination, dynamic projection mapping, and pyrotechnic acoustics.
DAY 08
✈️ Flight Home

Departure Back to India

●
Trip Outcomes: Hands-on robotics exposure, cross-cultural global mindset, and certified international STEM credentials.
●
Airport Transfer: Souvenir shopping at Narita/Haneda airport and homeward flight back to India.
